# OpsFlow

## Overview

OpsFlow is an AI-powered Accounts Payable Operations Platform.

The platform helps finance and operations teams automate invoice processing, compare invoices against Purchase Orders, detect discrepancies, and manage the exception resolution workflow before invoices are approved for payment.

OpsFlow combines document processing, workflow management, business rule validation, and AI to reduce manual invoice verification work.

---

# Problem Statement

Many organizations manually compare:

- Purchase Orders
- Vendor Invoices
- Goods Receipts

This process is slow, repetitive, and prone to human error.

Incorrect payments can occur due to:

- Incorrect prices
- Incorrect quantities
- Duplicate invoices
- Missing Purchase Orders
- Missing Goods Receipts

OpsFlow automates this verification process.
